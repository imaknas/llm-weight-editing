# Third-party material

- `analysis/iwi_editor_tools.json` — the four tool definitions sent to editor models, copied verbatim from the
  native traces published with IWIBench (https://github.com/HCLTech-robotics/iwibench, dataset
  `hcltech-robotics/iwibench` on Hugging Face; the repository is published under the MIT licence).
- The scripts under `analysis/` import the IWIBench package from a local checkout; no IWIBench source is included here.
- No Gemma weights and no Gemma-derived weight patches are included. They are available from their publishers under
  Gemma's terms.
